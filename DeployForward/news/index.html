DeployForward raises $100M+ and acquires Boosted to build Canada's sovereign AI deployment company

A Canadian-controlled, model-agnostic deployment company — anchored by the country's leading institutional capital, and built on the largest dedicated forward-deployed-engineering team in Canada.

TORONTO — June 8, 2026

DeployForward today announced more than $100M in committed capital — including Sagard, RBC, BDC and Constellation — and the acquisition of Boosted Technology, Canada's largest dedicated forward-deployed-engineering team. Together, the two moves stand up a sovereign-by-design AI deployment company purpose-built for the regulated middle of the Canadian economy.

The category was born this spring. The frontier labs are racing to own enterprise deployment — OpenAI absorbed its UK consulting partner to launch a US-domiciled, US-controlled Deployment Company, and a parallel Wall Street joint venture followed within the same week. Both are structurally unfit for the workloads where sovereignty matters most. Canada was not at the table. DeployForward is the answer.

Sovereign by design

Canadian data can't leave Canada. Banks, insurers, healthcare systems, and federal and provincial governments operate under strict residency and sovereignty constraints — and a US-controlled vendor will always be a check-box exercise here. DeployForward is a Canadian entity with Canadian leadership and Canadian-resident data, built to meet OSFI, PHIPA, PIPEDA and federal procurement standards out of the box.

The company is deliberately model-agnostic. It defaults to a Canadian model preference — Cohere and others — when they are at parity, with a standing commitment to switch back to Canadian models the moment they catch up. Where Canadian models aren't yet at parity, it deploys the best models in the world. As the team puts it internally: no religion, just outcomes. Every client gets the best model, every time.

"The category will be defined in the next 18 months. Canada gets one chance to be at the table — sovereign, model-agnostic, and anchored by the country's leading capital. That's the company we're building."

— Tom Taubkin, Chief Executive Officer, DeployForward

Built on Boosted, not bolted on

The acquisition collapses Boosted Technology into DeployForward at close — not a subsidiary, a clean roll-up. One brand, one P&L, a sovereign chassis from day one. Founded in Toronto in 2022 and previously CapitalG-backed, Boosted brings roughly 50 forward-deployed engineers and three-and-a-half years of real production deployments — the most credible operating chassis in the country for this work. Tom Taubkin continues as CEO; Esther Park continues as COO.

From there, the plan is organic: scaling the bench from 50 to 250 engineers over three years to become the de facto home for Canadian AI talent. This is not a roll-up of five firms — it is one team, one culture, one delivery standard. Because the company's institutional partners contribute a contracted revenue floor across their portfolio companies, DeployForward can hire ahead of demand against signed commitments rather than speculation.

Why these firms invested

The participating institutions are underwriting a single, two-sided return. They take equity in a new consulting firm at the moment a category is being created — and benefit as that firm appreciates while it scales from 50 to 250 engineers. At the same time, they secure priority access to what will be the most valuable resource for their organizations over the coming years: forward deployed engineers, embedded in their own portfolio companies, on sovereign terms.

Line one

Equity in an appreciating firm

Ownership in a Canadian-controlled deployment company at category inception — value compounding as the bench, the client book and the brand scale.

Line two

Securing the FDEs they'll need

Priority access to the country's deepest FDE bench for their own portfolio companies — capacity that turns AI pilots into production, at Canadian-sovereign data standards.

"Our partners win twice. They own a piece of a firm that appreciates as the category forms — and they lock in the engineering talent their portfolio companies will be fighting over for the next decade."

— Mystery Guest, Stakeholder Management, DeployForward

The participating institutions span strategic capital, a Canadian bank and customer, a sovereign-mandate development bank, and a distribution partner — a syndicate assembled to reflect both the returns case and the national mission behind the company.

About DeployForward

DeployForward is Canada's sovereign AI deployment company. It embeds forward deployed engineers inside enterprises and institutions to take AI from pilot to production — Canadian-controlled, Canadian model preference, best-in-class globally when parity isn't there yet. Built to meet OSFI, PHIPA, PIPEDA and federal procurement standards by default.

About Boosted

Boosted Technology is a Toronto-based applied-AI firm founded in 2022 and previously backed by CapitalG. With roughly 50 forward-deployed engineers, it is the largest dedicated FDE team in Canada and now operates as the founding chassis of DeployForward.
