What is forward deployed engineering?

How AI actually reaches production.

Forward deployed engineers (FDEs) are senior engineers we embed directly inside your organization — not a managed-service ticket queue, and not a generic product you're left to integrate. They sit with your people, learn the real problem, and build AI systems into the workflows your business actually runs on. It's the model the frontier labs now use to deploy AI in the real world, and it's how we take a project from pilot to production.
